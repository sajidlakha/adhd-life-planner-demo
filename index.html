Your day

Areas are used in task filters and in notes and reminders. Renaming here does not rename old tasks.

Your data

Saved in this browser only. Restore replaces, it does not merge.

Honest limits

No automatic sync. No reminders when the app is closed. No app-store install. This is an organising tool for personal use, not a medical device or treatment.
